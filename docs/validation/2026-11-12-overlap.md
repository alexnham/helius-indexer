The initial check exposed two conflicts: live persistence filtered out related
non-USDC transfers, while historical persistence kept the full decoded record
set. A sanitized captured regression was added, the paths were aligned, and
retained protobuf/block inputs were replayed offline into a fresh database.
Both arrival orders and repeated observations now coalesce without duplicates.
The original validation database is retained as evidence; its partial record sets
were not silently rewritten under the new rules.

The independent checker used Python integer/base58 parsing of successful classic
SPL transfer instructions, token-balance mint references for unchecked transfers,
and direct mint operands for checked transfers. It excluded transactions with
runtime failures, reconstructed outer/inner identities separately from the Rust
decoder, and compared IDs and raw amounts with stored results. It used the same
public RPC source as historical ingestion, so correlated provider omissions remain
possible. This is not an independent full-chain completeness certification.

The remaining missing edges are real coverage gaps, not a successful full index.
Whole-transaction rejection of unsupported cases, especially absent historical
Token-2022 fee evidence, can suppress otherwise recognizable USDC transfers in a
mixed transaction. The corrected replay still reports incomplete coverage for
all three slots. Historical replay found 141 decode failures across all block
transactions; these are not all USDC transfers. Initial live failure classes were
248 missing-fee-evidence cases, 29 incomplete-execution-evidence cases, nine
unsupported authority changes, and one unknown token instruction.

Local artifacts include the original report/events, retained SQLite inputs, the
fresh replay database, and independent comparison summaries under the ignored
validation data directory. The optional retained-input test documents replay
bounds in `../overlap.md`. Ordinary CI remains offline and does not require those
local captures or credentials.

Review regressions also verify that unresolved observations retain known block
time, blockhash, and token-balance evidence through sparse repairs. Compatible
pre/post ownership evidence is combined before updating decoded records. The
final offline suite passes 54 tests, with the retained-input replay separately
opted in; formatting and Clippy checks pass.
